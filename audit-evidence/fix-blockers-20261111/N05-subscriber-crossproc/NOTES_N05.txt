Chain exercised:
  1. Directory process installs a global V1 memory service and plants

  2. Ctl process registers two compute members, drives them to ACTIVE
     through the real membership route-commit + activate_member path,
     and binds wvm_ctl_subscriber_invalidate_bridge_bind.
  3. Ctl calls wvm_membership_controller_mark_monitor_health(...,
     UNREACHABLE) on 7:1001 — the production trigger for
     maybe_invalidate_subscribers_locked.
  4. The strong ctl hook captures membership and fans out
     WVM_ENVELOPE_MSG_INVALIDATE_SUBSCRIBERS (0x0307) over a Unix
     socket to the directory process. Fanout to the failed leaf has
     no listener (best-effort skip, failed=1).
  5. Directory decodes the 12-byte payload and calls
     wvm_memory_service_global_invalidate_subscriber_instance
     (same function the production admission_receiver 0x0307 case
     invokes). ACTIVE lease becomes STALE.
