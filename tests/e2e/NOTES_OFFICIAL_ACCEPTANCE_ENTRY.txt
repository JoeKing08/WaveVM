Live run, when requested
------------------------
Both VMs must have SharedFolder mounted, /dev/shm at 6G, and Tailscale
up (wavevm-f05-a / wavevm-f05-b). Start the same command on both sides,
node-b can wait for the join bundle. Example:

  ./scripts/wavevm f05 --role node-a --route-topology fractal
  ./scripts/wavevm f05 --role node-b --route-topology fractal
  ./scripts/wavevm f10 --role node-a --route-topology flat
  ./scripts/wavevm f10 --role node-b --route-topology flat
